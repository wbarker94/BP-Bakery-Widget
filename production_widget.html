Home screen production widget

Upload a production spreadsheet, such as the Catcher Sheet, and see how its numbers would appear in a widget on the WorkJam home screen.

Drop a spreadsheet here

Excel (.xlsx, .xls) or CSV

How this prototype reads the file

  • It looks for a header row with Product and Total columns. Per Screen, Full and Eaches are used when present.
  • The date comes from the cell next to "Date:".
  • Tabs without that layout show every row that has a number in it.
  • Stations, the Rondo dough plan and mix amounts are worked out from the Catcher Sheet totals using the same formulas as the Rondo and Cake Mixing tabs.
  • The file stays in your browser. Nothing is uploaded to a server.
